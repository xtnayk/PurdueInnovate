Behind the plan

What this prototype does, and what still needs validation.

Working in this demo

  • OR-Tools selects compatible, feasible candidate routes.
  • Each option has a computed schedule, cost and labor estimate.
  • 3D playback previews travel, spraying, weather and field progress without dispatching a rig.
  • The portable replay runs in an existing browser without installation.
  • Field windows, rainfast periods, wind, wetness and fixed crew constraints are checked.
  • A manager can approve a current plan locally and export review files.

Planned integrations

  • Gemini for proposed updates and grounded explanations.
  • WHIN / Purdue Mesonet observations and NWS forecasts.
  • Product-specific, reviewed application rules and road travel times.
  • SMS delivery and machine-specific ISOXML validation.

Model assumptions

    “Optimal” means optimal within the generated candidate routes. A rain buffer is the time between the end of the required dry period and modeled rain. It is not a runoff estimate, a safety guarantee or a probability.
